# src/chat

The chat with a coding agent, as the views an app mounts in its columns, in the palette's colors (`@light/ui`). `host.ts` is `ChatHost`, what the views need of the app (the conversation of the moment, the screen, the kernel, the roles, the folder's files, the message history), each a method an app answers over its own host. `chat-pane.ts` is `ChatPane`: the transcript (`ChatSource`, a `ModelSource`) with the composer under it, the busy line, the queue, the walk through the folder's messages, the completion strip (`suggest.ts`: paths after @, commands after /, their values) and what a line typed does (the chat role's `typed`). `request-panel.ts` draws the agent's questions, plans and permissions and sends the answer. `composer.ts` is the editing over light's input as actions in the "input" scope (undo, kill and yank, the clipboard). `tools-pane.ts` is the Tools tab: the calls of the conversation as rows (`ToolsSource`) in a `ToolsPanel`, a press opening a call in the code column. `roles.ts` holds `ChatTarget` and `ToolsTarget`, the roles other plugins reach the chat column and the Tools tab through.

Tests: widecode's chat, composer, editor, requests, queue, busy, tools and goldens tests prove the views over recorded sessions.
